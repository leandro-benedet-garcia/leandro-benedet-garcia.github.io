About Me

I am a Senior Gameplay Engineer with 13+ years of software development in total including 6+ years of experience working directly on games and interactive applications. I can work with existing Unity and Godot projects, implementing gameplay systems, networking, physics, optimization and difficult integrations without unnecessary rewrites. In addition to that I am capable of making games from the prototype to the final release.

My professional experience with game engines are Unity and C# and in Godot with GDScript, C# and C++.

My experience includes gameplay and game systems, networked multiplayer, authoritative dedicated servers, game AI, VR, physics, procedural generation and performance optimization. I have also worked as a project/technical lead, reviewing code and coordinating the work of other programmers.
